## Risk descriptions

Where a risk carries a longer description, it is held in the risk system. The
descriptions below are reproduced as recorded.

**R-02 — Regulatory censure following a cyber incident.** "Risk that a cyber
incident results in regulatory action by the PRA or FCA." Controls cited:
Sentinel SIEM, Defender for Endpoint, security policy suite, annual
penetration test.

**R-04 — Core banking outage causes customer detriment.** "Risk that
unavailability of the Corebridge platform prevents customers accessing
services." Controls cited: Corebridge SLA, DR arrangements, incident
management process. Score unchanged since June 2024 despite three Q1 2025
outages; the Q2 2025 review recorded that the score "remains appropriate".

**R-09 — Ransomware affecting the corporate estate.** "Risk of ransomware
deployment causing loss of availability and/or data." Controls cited: Defender
for Endpoint, email filtering, backups, user awareness training.

**R-11 — Key person dependency, platform team.** "Concentration of knowledge of
the legacy estate and migration in a small number of individuals." Controls
cited: documentation programme, cross-training. Raised January 2025 following
the departure of a senior platform engineer.

**R-17 — Failure to meet operational resilience impact tolerances.** "Risk that
an operational disruption exceeds a board-approved impact tolerance for an
important business service." Controls cited: 2025 self-assessment, DR testing,
incident management.

## Register notes

**Review process.** The Risk Committee reviews the register quarterly. Minutes
for the last four meetings record discussion of scores. They do not record any
risk being added, removed, or re-described in that period.

**Movement.** Seven of the eighteen risks have not moved since 2024. R-02 and
R-09 have not moved since February 2024.

**Ownership.** Every risk is owned at executive level. No risk is owned by the
Head of Internal Audit, the DPO, or the Head of Digital Workplace.

**Vacancy.** The Chief Risk Officer post has been vacant since May 2025. The
register is maintained by the Risk function (6 FTE) reporting to the COO in her
interim CRO capacity. Two of the eighteen risks are owned by the COO.

**Controls.** Controls are cited by name. There is no field recording whether a
cited control has been tested, when, or by whom.
