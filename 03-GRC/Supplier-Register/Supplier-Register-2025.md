## Register notes

**Purpose.** Built by Procurement in 2021 to manage contract renewals and
spend. Its fields were chosen for that purpose. It has not been re-scoped
since, and no other function has asked it to answer a different question.

**Maintenance.** Updated at contract signature and renewal. There is no
periodic review and no trigger when the scope of an existing engagement
changes.

**Criticality.** Assigned by the contract owner at onboarding. Twelve
suppliers are marked High, sixteen Medium, thirty-five Low. No supplier's
criticality has been changed since it was first assigned.

**Onboarding route.** Suppliers above £50,000 annual value go through
procurement with a due diligence questionnaire. Below that threshold, the
contract owner may engage directly using standard terms.

## Fields not held

- What data the supplier receives, and of what category
- Where the supplier processes it, and whether that is outside the UK
- Whether a data processing agreement exists
- Whether the arrangement is material outsourcing under PRA SS2/21
- Sub-processors and fourth parties
- Date of last due diligence
- Exit and substitution arrangements
- Whether the supplier supports an important business service
