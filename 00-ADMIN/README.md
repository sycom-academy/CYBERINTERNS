## Criteria live here. Results do not.

The rubric and marking scheme are **deliberately public**: interns should know
how they are being assessed before they are assessed, and publishing the
criteria is what makes the marks defensible.

What does not belong here is anything about an identifiable person — scores,
written feedback, moderation notes, certificates. Those are personal data and
live in the private `sycom-academy/SYCOM-INTERNSHIP-ASSESSMENT` repository,
which interns cannot access.

The line is: **criteria public, results private.**
