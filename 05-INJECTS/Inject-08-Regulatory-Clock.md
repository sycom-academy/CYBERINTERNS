## What you are asked to produce

**GRC team.**

**Notification assessment** → `06-DELIVERABLES/GRC/Notification-Assessment.md`

**1. The awareness date.** State it, to the hour, and defend it. Set out the
candidate dates, what was known at each, and the test you are applying. This
single decision drives every deadline below, so it carries the most weight of
anything you write this week.

**2. ICO.** Is Article 33 engaged. If yes, the deadline as a date and time, and
whether it has passed. If it has passed, say so plainly and say what you do
about that — a late notification is a different problem from a missed one.

**3. Data subjects.** Is Article 34 engaged. Apply the high-risk test properly.
If your answer is no, be specific about what would change it.

**4. PRA and FCA.** Whether this is notifiable under Principle 11 and SUP 15,
whether it engages the operational resilience framework, and whether the
settlement failure on W3 D1 is separately relevant. The CISO is right that this
is not only a data protection question.

convenience or split them for tidiness.

**6. A draft notification.** Write the ICO notification you would submit on the
facts as they stand. Where you do not know something, write what you would
actually put in the box — regulators expect incomplete initial notifications
and expect you to say what you are still establishing.

## Note on Artefact 2

The Chief Executive's email is not improper and you should not treat it as if
it were. He asks a legitimate question, states he will notify if the threshold
is met, and asks for a clear view instead of a hedge. All of that is
reasonable.

He has also framed the question in a way that invites a particular answer, at a
commercially sensitive moment, and asked you not to hedge about something
genuinely uncertain.

Your job is to give him the correct answer, clearly, including the parts he
will not like. If the threshold is met, say so and say why in terms he can act
on. If it is not met, say that too — reaching for notification because it feels
safer is its own failure of judgement.

How you handle this email is assessed.
