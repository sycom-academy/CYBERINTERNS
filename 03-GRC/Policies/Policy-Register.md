## Notes

**Approval route.** Policies P-01 to P-14 are approved by the board on
recommendation from the relevant committee, except P-11, which is a standard
approved by the CISO.

**Review discipline.** The annual review cycle is tracked by Compliance on a
spreadsheet. Overdue policies are reported in the quarterly Compliance report
to the Risk Committee. The Q2 2025 report listed four overdue; the Q3 report
listed six. No policy has been withdrawn or archived.

**P-04 — Data Retention Schedule.** Drafted by the DPO in 2024 to address
audit finding IA-2024-11. Never approved. The operative retention position in
the absence of an approved schedule is the tenant-wide 7-year rule configured
in Microsoft 365, which was set by the Digital Workplace team, not by the DPO.

**Attestation.** Staff attest annually to P-02 Acceptable Use as part of
mandatory training. There is no attestation requirement for any other policy.

## Policies not held

No policy or standard currently exists covering:

- Privileged access management *(audit finding IA-2024-07 refers)*
- Cryptography and key management
- Secure development and secure coding
- Cloud security configuration
- Logging, monitoring and detection
- Data classification and handling *(sensitivity labels are deployed without a
  supporting policy)*
- Use of non-production environments and test data
- Artificial intelligence and machine learning
