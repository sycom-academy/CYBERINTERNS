## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out FinServe Digital Bank Ltd's approach to information
security. It establishes management's commitment to protecting the
confidentiality, integrity and availability of the Bank's information assets.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors and third parties who access
the Bank's information or information systems.

## 3. Policy statements

### 3.1 Governance

The Bank shall maintain an information security function with appropriate
resources and authority. The Chief Information Security Officer is responsible
for the development and maintenance of this policy and shall report on
information security matters to the Risk Committee.

### 3.2 Risk management

Information security risks shall be identified, assessed and managed in
accordance with the Bank's risk management framework. Risks shall be recorded
in the risk register and reviewed periodically.

### 3.3 Access control

Access to information systems shall be granted on the principle of least
privilege and shall be reviewed periodically. Users shall be individually
identifiable and shall authenticate before being granted access. Privileged
access shall be restricted to authorised personnel.

### 3.4 Asset management

Information assets shall be identified and recorded in an asset register.
Asset owners shall be designated and shall be responsible for the appropriate
protection of their assets.

### 3.5 Human resources security

Personnel shall be subject to appropriate screening before employment.
All staff shall receive information security awareness training on joining and
annually thereafter.

### 3.6 Physical and environmental security

Information processing facilities shall be protected by appropriate physical
controls.

### 3.7 Operations security

Systems shall be protected against malware. Logs shall be retained. Changes to
systems shall be subject to change management. Backups shall be taken and
protected.

### 3.8 Supplier relationships

Suppliers with access to the Bank's information shall be subject to appropriate
due diligence and contractual provisions.

### 3.9 Incident management

Information security incidents shall be reported, recorded and investigated in
accordance with the Incident Management Policy.

### 3.10 Business continuity

Information security requirements shall be considered within business
continuity planning.

### 3.11 Compliance

The Bank shall comply with applicable legal, regulatory and contractual
requirements relating to information security.

## 4. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Board | Approves this policy |
| Risk Committee | Oversees information security risk |
| Chief Information Security Officer | Maintains this policy; reports on security matters |
| Chief Technology Officer | Implements technical controls |
| All staff | Comply with this policy and report incidents |

## 5. Compliance with this policy

Failure to comply with this policy may result in disciplinary action.

## 6. Review

This policy shall be reviewed annually, or sooner following a material change
to the Bank's operations, technology or regulatory environment.
