## What you are asked to produce

**Both teams, one pack.** This is a joint deliverable and it will be assessed
as one document, not as two halves stapled together.

→ `07-FINAL/Board-Pack/`

**1. Executive summary** — one page. Answers the three questions. A reader who
stops here should be able to make the decisions you are asking for.

**2. What we found** — the assessment. Ten to fifteen pages. Findings, each
with: what it is, evidence, what could happen, and how likely. Prioritised by
consequence. Pool the two workstreams — a finding that appears in both is one
finding, not two.

**3. The incident** — narrative and timeline. Length is your judgement, and
that judgement is part of what is assessed. Say what happened, what you could
establish, and what you could not — including where the reason you could not is
a control gap rather than a limit of the investigation.

**4. Remediation plan** — what to do, in order. Each item: the finding it
addresses, indicative effort, dependencies, and what carrying the risk for
another quarter means instead. Distinguish what is fixable in thirty days from
what is a programme.

**5. Regulatory position** — the notification decisions, the basis, and what
remains open. Written so that if the ICO or the FCA read it, it would not
embarrass the bank.

**6. Appendices** — registers, detection surface, coverage gaps, the DPIA. The
supporting evidence, so the body of the pack does not have to carry it.

## Format

Markdown in `07-FINAL/Board-Pack/`. One file per section, numbered, plus an
`README.md` that acts as the contents page.

Every factual claim in the pack traces to something in
`06-DELIVERABLES/` or `02-ARCHITECTURE/`. If you cannot point at the source,
either find it or take the claim out.
