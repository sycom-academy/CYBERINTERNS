## Part 2: What you are being asked

Sycom Academy runs this programme in a GitHub repository that will be public,
readable by anyone on the internet, including search engines and future
employers.

If you take part publicly, the following become permanently world-readable:

- Every document you write during the programme
- Every commit message, pull request and review comment you write
- The author name and email recorded in your git commits
- Your GitHub account, and the times of day you worked

This cannot be undone. Other people can copy the repository, search engines
cache it, and archive sites keep copies. If you later change your mind, Sycom
Academy can make its own copy private or delete it, but cannot remove copies
held by anyone else. Please be sure you understand this before choosing.

Your marks, feedback and certificate are never published in any case. They
are held privately and can be deleted on request.

## Part 3: Your choice

Choose one. Your assessment, feedback, mentor time and certificate are
identical in all three. No option is better regarded than another and your
choice is not shared with other interns.

- [ ] Mode A: Attributed. My work is public under my real name and my own
      GitHub account. I understand this is permanent and cannot be reversed.

- [ ] Mode B: Pseudonymous. *(default)* My work is public, but under a
      pseudonymous account not linked to my name. I can prove the work is mine
      to an employer privately, whenever I choose. I understand the work itself
      is permanent.

- [ ] Mode C: Private. My work is not published. It stays in a private
      repository seen only by my mentors. I understand I may choose to publish
      later, and that this is the only option I can change my mind about.

*If you return this form without a box ticked, you will be placed in Mode B.*

## Part 6: How to return this

Send the completed form privately to the programme lead, by email or any
private channel you already use with them. A photograph or scan of a printed
copy is fine. So is a typed version: the words matter, the format does not.

Do not:

| Do not | Why |
|---|---|
| Commit it to this repository | It is public. Your mode would be readable by anyone, permanently |
| Open a pull request containing it | Same, and you have no write access yet, nobody does before their form is recorded |
| Send it to a mentor | Mentors are told which repository you work in and nothing else. Your choice is not theirs to know |
| Send it in a group email or shared thread | Your answer is individual and private. `CONSENT-AND-PUBLICATION.md` §5.3 |

The programme lead files it in the private assessment repository. You will not
have access to that repository and are not meant to.

If you do not return a form, you are placed in Mode B: pseudonymous.
Nobody will chase you into a different answer, and silence is never read as
agreement to publish under your real name.

## Withdrawing or changing your mind

Contact the programme lead at any time.

| You want to | Possible? |
|---|---|
| See what is held about you | Yes, in full |
| Delete your marks and feedback | Yes, in full |
| Move from Mode C to A or B | Yes |
| Move from A or B to Mode C | Sycom's own copy can be made private or deleted. Copies already taken by others cannot be recalled |
| Correct something published | Only by adding a correction. The original stays in the history and in any fork |

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at `ico.org.uk`.
